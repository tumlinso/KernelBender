# Choose the experiment that changes the decision

A candidate can answer three different questions. Do not confuse their evidence.

**Feasibility:** Does the equation/map implement the required operator, and can the supported hardware form express it? Use small exact identities, one-hot maps, range proofs or a legal compile/runtime smoke. Finite agreement does not prove arbitrary floating error bounds or memory-model legality.

**Mechanism:** Which cost accounts for a divergence? Compare a changed component while preserving enough of the rest to make the attribution meaningful. A whole redesign can establish a combined effect without isolating its individual levers. State that limit instead of demanding a complete factorial ablation every time.

**Advantage:** Is the complete caller-visible work better under the same semantic, numerical and environment contract? Use the KDA evaluator and unprofiled measurements. Profile replay durations and architecture peaks do not replace this test. [KDA-ENGINE, KDA-ATTRIBUTION, NCU-VOLTA]

## Before spending GPU time

Write the current decision, the competing explanations, what remains fixed, what changes and the result that would reject the candidate or hypothesis. Use existing evidence first. A test that would not change any design or knowledge claim is probably unnecessary.

Keep the strongest valid baseline. Reuse its input/oracle infrastructure, but repair known correctness hazards before making fresh performance comparisons. A baseline repair has a new identity; historical timing does not transfer automatically. Candidate code must not edit the trusted validator, input corpus or tolerances.

For new representations include encode, maintenance, decode and required materialization. Report a steady-state view separately when meaningful, with its actual reuse/invalidation assumptions. Memory footprint is not measured memory traffic; nominal arithmetic is not useful arithmetic; one-run uncertainty is not cross-machine generalization.

## Keep exploration broad enough, but bounded

Prefer a few structurally different constructions over many cosmetic variants. A speculative branch can be valuable for information even if it does not win, provided its test is cheap and answers a consequential question. Do not demand that each individual step be faster when the proposed gain occurs over a composition.

Change the design family when new evidence warrants it. Keep local tuning in the existing CUDA/KDA workflow once formulation and ownership are settled. Stop when a useful winner, conventional choice or explanatory rejection is supported. Do not turn unresolved microarchitecture into an unrelated benchmark campaign.

## Close the loop with a reusable lesson

Record the exact transformation and preserved operator, applicability and access, input/lifetime regime, observation, bounded causal claim and remaining uncertainty. Link the source/binary and real report. A rejected candidate should say which assumption failed, not “bitsets are slow” or “fusion is bad.”

Propose a reviewed addition to the relevant knowledge owner. KernelWiki remains the place for validated implementations/facts; KernelBender may retain a short transferable design example; the Atlas remains a continuing independent research source. Do not auto-promote hypotheses to verified facts or update all repositories at once.
