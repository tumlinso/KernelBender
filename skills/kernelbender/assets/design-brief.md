# KernelBender design brief

**Disposition:** propose / probe / conventional / blocked-contract
**Task/target/evidence identity:** refer to the caller's existing contract and sources.

## What is actually required

State the operator and observable output; legal invariants, numerical obligations, lifetime/reuse and the cost boundary. Name the inherited convention being challenged. List only consequential unknowns.

## Recommended construction

Give an equation or compact pseudocode and trace semantic values through representation, ownership and consumer use. Identify eliminated work and newly introduced work. State the exact relevant hardware/API contract or the check needed to establish it.

## Serious alternative and economic boundary

Retain the strongest conventional implementation or another structurally distinct design. Explain preparation, repeated use, updates, extraction, workspace and interference. Do not fabricate precise speed estimates; use bounds/regimes when that is all the evidence supports.

## First discriminating experiment

What decision does it resolve? What remains fixed? What changes? Which result rejects the candidate or explanation? State the independent correctness test and full objective measurement. Distinguish a feasibility probe from a causal comparison and a promotion test.

## Handoff and limits

Next implementation/probe action, relevant source locators, baseline/parent identity, evidence class and remaining uncertainty. The calling KDA agent owns candidate source, validation, execution and promotion. Do not repeat the entire design history.
